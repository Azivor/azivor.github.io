# Azivor editorial redesign
Scope: Home content arrival, Explore, Builds, project notebook, About. Visitor modes: Experience for Builds/Home, Read for Explore/About. The user chose an editorial magazine with varied layouts and stronger storytelling, built directly as working pages.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Lead with real work, then offer short choices into deeper reading; replace repeated heading-plus-box chapters.
OWN-WORLD: Existing sky/nav identity, Geist reading typography, navy #143b59, cloud white #f4f8fc, cyan #d6ebf7. Real project captures, open typographic sections, thin separators, 14px image corners.
STORY: Discover the actual project; choose a notebook, an exercise, or a workflow; inspect only the depth you request.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Builds uses a compact masthead above a large artifact and its title/actions. Explore uses a larger headline and unequal story entries. About centers a two-line statement. Home keeps Earth and arrives at a lead story with two short side entries.
FORM: Editorial magazine pinned by the user's answer; seed 41bd3fc7 explored alternatives. Signature interaction is a short teaser opening its matching native disclosure, including deep links and repeat activation. No decorative entrance motion.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

References: Hack Club's real maker work and varied visual density; Experiments with Google's browse/detail separation; Are.na's compact linked entry points. No borrowed artwork or code.
