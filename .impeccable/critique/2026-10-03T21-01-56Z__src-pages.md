## Overall judgment

Azivor’s Earth opening feels distinctive and authored. The information pages lose that distinction because Explore, Builds, and About repeat the same oversized introduction and boxed prose. The biggest opportunity is to give each page a composition suited to its purpose: an exercise on Explore, an inspectable artifact on Builds, and a concise explanation on About.

The blue palette, readable typography, generous touch targets, section links, and honest discussion of limitations work well. Keep the cinematic introduction and requested boxes. Improve how quickly visitors reach useful material and how clearly those boxes communicate different roles.

## Changes to make

**[P1] Fix the mobile menu’s keyboard behavior.** After opening the menu, four Tab presses reach a covered page link; Enter activates it while the overlay stays open. Keep focus inside the menu, make background content inactive, and return focus when closing. Verify Tab, Shift+Tab, Escape, and link activation. Suggested command: `$impeccable harden`.

**[P1] Make the first exercise an obvious destination.** The homepage’s Explore journey leads to categories, while Explore places its exercise after substantial explanation. Add a clearly named “Try the first exercise” action at the homepage content arrival and near Explore’s introduction. Preserve the Earth sequence. Suggested command: `$impeccable onboard`.

**[P2] Shorten and differentiate interior headers.** At 390×844, Builds’ first screen contains its heading and fade but no project detail. Reduce the space reserved for the transition and bring meaningful content forward. Use a task-led Explore opening, artifact-led Builds opening, and quieter About opening. Suggested commands: `$impeccable layout`, `$impeccable adapt`.

**[P2] Make Builds’ evidence inspectable.** Its screenshot and narrative explain the project but provide little concrete revision evidence. Show a genuine before/after or annotated decision with the observation, human choice, and check performed. Use actual project material. Suggested command: `$impeccable shape`.

**[P2] Give the boxes distinct jobs.** The Human Loop’s steps resemble feature cards, while exercise instructions have similar visual weight to supporting text. Retain the bento structure, but distinguish sequence, prompt, explanation, and caveat through restrained surface colors, heading scale, and spacing. Make the exercise prompt easy to identify and copy. Suggested commands: `$impeccable distill`, `$impeccable layout`.

## Visitor risks and detector evidence

A first-time visitor can understand the philosophy before finding a concrete starting task. A phone visitor must pass a nearly full-screen introduction before reaching substantive material. A visitor checking credibility sees honest limitations but little inspectable evidence of how the build changed. Cognitive load is moderate: hierarchy, one clear decision, and progressive disclosure need work. Navigation does not show a demonstrated excess of choices. The emotional peak is the Earth opening; repetitive interior introductions create a valley afterward.

The detector reported **37 signals: 30 warnings and 7 advisories**, across the four generated Home, Explore, Builds, and About pages. These are not 37 confirmed defects. Padding and tracking warnings were contradicted by rendered styles; uppercase warnings referred to short metadata. Geist, glass, glow, and numbered labels require design judgment rather than automatic removal. Five contrast signals remain candidates: gradient text needs measurement against the actual pixels behind it before claiming a failure. The current footer’s dark text was not the white text implicated by those signals.

Smaller refinements: align “Workflows” with “Human Loop,” and clarify the difference between Explore as a homepage action and Explore as a page. The footer is lower priority than content hierarchy.
